# Plug-ins that help with accessibility

Three plug-ins are made for accessibility: two for blind and partially sighted people who watch with audio
description, and one with a style for reading with low vision.

## When would you use it

- Someone in your home is blind or partially sighted and watches with audio description.
- You want larger, stronger text and focus rings across Starboard.

## The plug-ins

| Plug-in | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Audio description](../../audio-description/README.md) | Finds films and series in Jellyfin with a described sound track, lists them, and prefers releases with one in Reelix searches |
| [Audio description tracks](../../audio-description-tracks/README.md) | Adds a description track you already have, such as one from AudioVault, to its film or episode, after checking the lengths match |
| [Styles](../../styles/README.md) | Its **Large and clear** style has strong contrast, heavy headings and wide focus rings |

## Things to note

- Audio description is a second sound track in which a narrator says what is happening in the picture. In Jellyfin,
  choose it from the sound tracks while a film plays.
- **High contrast** in Starboard's **Display** always wins over any style.
