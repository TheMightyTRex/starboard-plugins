# VPN check: Settings

VPN check's settings are on its own tab: go to **Settings**, then **Plug-ins**, then **VPN check**. Starboard draws them and keeps them; secrets are never shown again once saved.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Your home's public address** | Optional, such as 81.2.69.160. If the VPN ever shows this address, the card turns red. Words. | Empty |
| **Check every** | A number from 5 to 240 minutes. | 15 minutes |

## Buttons

These are on VPN check's tab.

- **Check now**

## Things to note

- A setting you change is used straight away; you do not need to deactivate it first.
- Settings are kept when you update or deactivate it, and when you remove it they stay in its folder.
