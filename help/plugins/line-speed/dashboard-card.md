# Line speed: Dashboard card

Line speed adds 2 cards you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Line speed's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Line speed

Shows a number, with a few words under it.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** 72 Mbps, 04:00 today

## Line speed this week

Shows a short list, up to 10 lines.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:**
  - 72 Mbps: Wednesday 04:00

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Line speed), such as **Line speed (Line speed)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Line speed must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
