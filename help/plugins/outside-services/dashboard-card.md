# Outside services: Dashboard card

Outside services adds 2 cards you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Outside services's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Outside services

Shows one line saying whether all is well.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** All 4 answer

## Outside services

Shows a short list, up to 10 lines.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:**
  - Router: Up, 4 ms

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Outside services), such as **Outside services (Outside services)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Outside services must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
