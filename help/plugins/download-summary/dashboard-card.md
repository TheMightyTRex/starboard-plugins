# Download summary: Dashboard card

Download summary adds 1 card you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Download summary's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Downloaded today

Shows a number, with a few words under it.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** 12.88 GB today, 41 GB this week

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Download summary), such as **Downloaded today (Download summary)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Download summary must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
