# Picture shrinker: Dashboard card

Picture shrinker adds 1 card you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Picture shrinker's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Space saved

Shows a number, with a few words under it.

- **How often it updates:** every 1 minute while the dashboard is open and in view. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** 41.20 GB saved, 18,402 pictures shrunk. Next run Sunday 16 November at 03:30

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Picture shrinker), such as **Space saved (Picture shrinker)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Picture shrinker must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
