# Picture shrinker: Settings

Picture shrinker's settings are on its own tab: go to **Settings**, then **Plug-ins**, then **Picture shrinker**. Starboard draws them and keeps them; secrets are never shown again once saved.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Space saved** | A page of its own on Picture shrinker's tab. | None |
| **When to shrink** | Every day, week or month, at a time. It works at the lowest priority, so a time when the server is quiet is best. Every day, week or month, at a time. | Every week on Sunday at 03:30 |
| **Posters no taller than** | A poster taller than this is shrunk to this height. 900 pixels looks sharp on a television. A number from 300 to 3000 pixels. | 900 pixels |
| **Photos of people no wider than** | A photo of a cast or crew member wider than this is shrunk to this width. A number from 100 to 2000 pixels. | 400 pixels |
| **Shrink new pictures in quiet hours** | Each hour of quiet hours, shrinks the posters and photos Jellyfin saved since the last pass, so they never build up. Activated or deactivated. | Deactivated |

## Buttons

These are on Picture shrinker's tab.

- **Shrink now**
- **Stop shrinking**

## Things to note

- A setting you change is used straight away; you do not need to deactivate it first.
- Settings are kept when you update or deactivate it, and when you remove it they stay in its folder.
