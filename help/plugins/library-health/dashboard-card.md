# Library health: Dashboard card

Library health adds 2 cards you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Library health's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Library health

Shows a number, with a few words under it.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** 4 to fix, of 1,240 titles

## To fix in Jellyfin

Shows a short list, up to 10 lines.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:**
  - Brazil (1985): No poster

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Library health), such as **Library health (Library health)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Library health must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
