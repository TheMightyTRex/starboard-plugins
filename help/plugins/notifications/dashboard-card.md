# Notifications: Dashboard card

Notifications adds 2 cards you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Notifications's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Notifications

Shows one line saying whether all is well.

- **How often it updates:** every 1 hour while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** Mon 09:00, New this week: sent by Email, Telegram

## Notifications sent

Shows a short list, up to 10 lines.

- **How often it updates:** every 1 hour while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:**
  - Ready to watch: Dune (2021): Mon 12 Nov 20:15, sent by Email, Telegram

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Notifications), such as **Notifications (Notifications)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Notifications must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
