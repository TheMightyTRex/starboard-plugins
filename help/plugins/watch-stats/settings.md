# Watch statistics: Settings

Watch statistics's settings are on its own tab: go to **Settings**, then **Plug-ins**, then **Watch statistics**. Starboard draws them and keeps them; secrets are never shown again once saved.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Recent plays** | A page of its own on Watch statistics's tab. | None |
| **Top titles** | A page of its own on Watch statistics's tab. | None |
| **People** | A page of its own on Watch statistics's tab. | None |
| **Over the last** | A number from 1 to 365 days. | 30 days |
| **Leave out these people** | One Jellyfin user name a line, such as an account for testing. One a line. | Empty |

## Buttons

These are on Watch statistics's tab.

- **Read the figures again now**

## Things to note

- A setting you change is used straight away; you do not need to deactivate it first.
- Settings are kept when you update or deactivate it, and when you remove it they stay in its folder.
