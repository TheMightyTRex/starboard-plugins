# Seerr: Settings

Seerr has no settings in Starboard. Everything is set in the app itself, on its own page: open it with **Open Seerr** on its tab.

## Buttons

Seerr's tab, in **Settings**, then **Plug-ins**, then **Seerr**, has these buttons:

- **Open Seerr** opens its own page in a new tab. It is there while it is active.
- **Activate** starts it, and Starboard chooses a free port for its page. **Deactivate** stops it and keeps its folder.
- **Try again** starts it again when it did not start.
- **Look for an update** is there when this Starboard is newer than the version was made for.
- **Remove Seerr** stops it and takes it away. You choose where its folder goes; nothing is deleted.

## Things to note

- Reelix already has requests in Starboard, in **Downloads**, **Requests**. If you use both, choose one place for people to ask.
- Seerr reaches Jellyfin on this computer at `http://host.docker.internal:8096`.
- Seerr keeps its users, requests and choices in its own folder in Starboard's data, `data/plugins/seerr`.
- Its settings are kept when you update or deactivate it, and when you remove it they stay in its folder.
