# Kavita: Settings

Kavita has no settings of its own. Everything is set in the app itself, on its own page.

Kavita has no buttons on its tab. Its tab shows its state, its versions, **Its web page** with **Open Kavita**, and what it may do.

## Things to note

- Kavita reads `books` and `comics` in your media folder, read only, so it never changes or deletes a file.
- Its accounts and settings are kept in its own folder, `data/plugins/kavita`. Its settings file there, `appsettings.json`, is written once before Kavita first starts, with a sign-in key made for this install, and never written over afterwards.
- Starboard chooses its port, 5000 when it is free, and shows it in **Its web page**.
- The main install can also add Kavita, in **Install apps**, **Ebooks**. Use one or the other.
- Everything it keeps stays when you update or deactivate it, and when you remove it, it stays in its folder.
