# Most watched: Dashboard card

Most watched adds 1 card you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Most watched's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Most watched

Shows a short list, up to 10 lines.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:**
  - Slow Horses: 3 people, 11 plays

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Most watched), such as **Most watched (Most watched)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Most watched must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
