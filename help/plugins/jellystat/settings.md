# Jellystat: Settings

Jellystat's settings are on its own tab: go to **Settings**, then **Plug-ins**, then **Jellystat**. Starboard draws them and keeps them; secrets are never shown again once saved.

## Restore a Jellystat backup

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Backup file to restore** | Only if you have one: the name of a pg_dump file you put in the restore folder, such as jfstat.pgdump. Words. | Empty |

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Restore a Jellystat backup** | Brings in the figures from a Jellystat you had before. Optional. A page of its own on Jellystat's tab. | None |

## Buttons

These are on Jellystat's tab.

- **Restore the backup**

## Things to note

- A setting you change is used straight away; you do not need to deactivate it first.
- Settings are kept when you update or deactivate it, and when you remove it they stay in its folder.
