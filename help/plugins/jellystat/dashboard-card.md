# Jellystat: Dashboard card

Jellystat adds 1 card you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Jellystat's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Jellystat

Shows one line saying whether all is well.

- **How often it updates:** every 10 minutes while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** Connected to Jellyfin. Open Jellystat from its page in Settings, Plug-ins.

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Jellystat), such as **Jellystat (Jellystat)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Jellystat must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
