## Containers

| Container | Network | Folders | Runs as | Starts | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `main` | `default` | `data` | `10001:10001` | `always` | 48 MB, 0.1 core |

## What Starboard calls

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `GET /health` | Health: 200 while it can work |
| `GET /card` | The Disk forecast card (status) |
