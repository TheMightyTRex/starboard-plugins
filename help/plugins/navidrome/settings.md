# Navidrome: Settings

Navidrome has no settings of its own. Everything is set in the app itself, on its own page.

Navidrome has no buttons on its tab. Its tab shows its state, its versions, **Its web page** with **Open Navidrome**, and what it may do.

## Things to note

- Navidrome reads `music` in your media folder, read only, so it never changes or deletes a file.
- Its accounts and settings are kept in its own folder, `data/plugins/navidrome`.
- Starboard chooses its port, 4533 when it is free, and shows it in **Its web page**.
- Jellyfin also plays music. Navidrome is for Subsonic apps on phones and in the car.
- Everything it keeps stays when you update or turn it off, and when you remove it, it stays in its folder.
