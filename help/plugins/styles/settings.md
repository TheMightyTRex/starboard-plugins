# Styles: Settings

Styles has no settings of its own, and no buttons. You choose one of its 22 styles in **Display**: select **Display** in the header, then choose under **Style**.

## Things to note

- **Style** shows in the Display panel only while Styles, or another plug-in with styles, is active.
- Your choice of style is kept in the browser you use, so each phone, computer and browser has its own. Choose it again on each one.
- **High contrast**, text size, wider spacing, underlined links and reduced motion in **Display** always win over a style.
- 17 styles change every part of Starboard: cards, buttons, tabs, the header, the side menu and chips. They are Mica, Dusk, Liquid glass, Brutal, Bento, Almanac, Garden, Terminal, Riso, Vantage, Newsprint, Piste, Studio, Expressive, Swiss, Neon and Clay.
- The other 5, Aurora, Sunset, Winter, Large and clear and Classic, keep Starboard's own cards, buttons and tabs, and change colours, corners, fonts, shadows and, in some, the page's gradient, a texture and glass panels.
- A style changes colours, corner shapes, the font (from fonts already on your device), shadows, the page's gradient, a light texture, glass panels and, in the 17, how cards, buttons, tabs, the header, the side menu and chips are drawn. It cannot change anything else.
- In version 1.6.0, 11 styles have new names and designs. If you chose one of the old ones, your choice moves to its new design by itself: Midnight is now Dusk, Frost is Liquid glass, Bold is Brutal, Bento keeps its name, Paper is Almanac, Forest is Garden, Retro is Terminal, Vivid is Riso, Graphite is Vantage, Editorial is Newsprint and Nordic is Piste.
- To go back to the standard style, select **Reset style** under the list of styles in **Display**.
