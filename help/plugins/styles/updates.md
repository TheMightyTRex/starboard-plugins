## What is new in 1.6.0

Changed:

- **Eleven styles redesigned in full:** Dusk, Liquid glass, Brutal, Bento, Almanac, Garden, Terminal, Riso, Vantage, Newsprint and Piste now change cards, buttons, tabs, the header, the side menu and chips, not only colours.
- **They replace the old eleven:** Midnight is now Dusk, Frost is Liquid glass, Bold is Brutal, Bento keeps its name, Paper is Almanac, Forest is Garden, Retro is Terminal, Vivid is Riso, Graphite is Vantage, Editorial is Newsprint and Nordic is Piste. Anyone using one of the old ones moves to its new design, with nothing to choose again.

## What is new in 1.5.0

Added:

- **Classic:** Starboard's design before Mica (teal on cool blue-grey, square corners), now that Mica is Starboard's own style.

Changed:

- **Mica first:** Mica, now Starboard's own design, is the first style, with the same colours as the design system's default.
- Every style keeps its own design on top of the new Mica design system: cards lift only in styles with elevated cards, and the hover on menus has a clear edge.

## How an update is kept safe

- Styles runs no container, so there is nothing to stop or wait for: Starboard checks the new version's styles and installs it.
- Your settings and its folder are kept.

## Update it

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Plug-ins**. When a newer version is known, Styles has an **Update** chip in **Manage**.
2. Select **Styles** to open its tab.
3. In **Versions**, select **Update to** and the new version.
4. Read **What is new since** your version and **How the update is kept safe**, enter your Starboard password, and select **Update Styles**.
5. When the dialog says it is updated, select **Done**.

## Roll it back

1. On Styles' tab, in **Versions**, select **Roll back to** and the version you had.
2. Enter your Starboard password and select **Roll back**.
3. When the dialog says it is back on that version, select **Done**.

Rolling back needs no download, and only one earlier version is kept.
