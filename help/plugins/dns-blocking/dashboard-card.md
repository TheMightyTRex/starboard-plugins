# Ad blocking: Dashboard card

Ad blocking adds 1 card you can put on your dashboard. Each starts with a grey band: Ad blocking's logo and name, a status in words, and when it last changed.

## Blocked today

Shows a number, with a few words under it.

- **How often it updates:** once a minute while the dashboard is open. Nothing is asked while the browser tab is hidden.
- **Example:** 4,210 blocked, 12% of 35,000 lookups

## Add it to your dashboard

1. Select **Edit dashboard**.
2. In **Add a card**, choose the card. Its name ends with (Ad blocking), such as **Blocked today (Ad blocking)**.
3. Select **Save**.

Ad blocking must be added and active first. Read [Install](install.md).
