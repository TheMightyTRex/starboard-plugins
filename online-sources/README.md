# Online sources (private draft)

Find free pictures and music, keep who made them and the licence, and send a pack to Create your own TV channel. Not in the
catalogue and not listed in the store: add it with **Upload a zip file** in Settings, Plug-ins. Needs Starboard with plug-in API 1.25.

- **Find a picture** and **Find music**: enter a few words. Openverse needs no key. Pexels needs its free key in the settings.
  Starboard makes every request, only to the sites this plug-in lists, and keeps a record on its page under **What it fetched**.
  Only items free for any use are shown unless you turn on **Show items I may not be able to use**.
- **Save as**: choose where a result goes in the pack (the Coming up next background, the promo background or promo music). It is kept with its
  maker, licence and where it came from.
- **Your pack**: what you saved. **Send your pack to Create your own TV channel** puts a zip with a LICENCE.txt (where each file came
  from) in a shared folder. Create your own TV channel checks it before it uses it.
- **Styles from the library**: styles other people made, MIT licensed, with credits, listed only after they were read. A style listed as
  a link only is never downloaded.
- **This page is for a computer screen. It is not made for phones.**

Music from Jamendo is never fetched (its terms do not allow keeping its music on a server). Nothing runs until you press something.
