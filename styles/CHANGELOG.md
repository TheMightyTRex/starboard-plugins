# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (12 November 2026)

### Added
- Six styles that reskin the whole look, from current design trends: Studio (a quiet product look with hairline
  borders), Expressive (tonal colour blocks and pill buttons), Swiss (white, black and red, heavy rules and capital
  labels), Neon (synthwave glows on a grid, with a gradient button), Clay (puffy soft-3D panels) and Mica (a calm
  desktop look with see-through menus).
- They use Starboard's new style settings: card, button, border, tab, header, side menu and chip styles, a primary
  button gradient, capital labels and letter spacing, and clay and hairline shadows. Every colour pair still passes
  WCAG 2.2 contrast, and High contrast still wins.

### Changed
- Needs Starboard's plug-in API 1.2 (the Starboard release after 0.16.5).

## 1.2.0 (12 November 2026)

### Added
- Winter: pine green and berry red over a frosty page, with falling-snow dots, soft glows, glass panels, rounded
  buttons and fields, and rounded headings.
- Large and clear: strong contrast, heavy headings, wide focus rings, layered depth and a calm gradient, with no
  glass or texture, so reading stays easy.
- Both set the side menu, the chosen button, count badges, status messages, chart colours and the logo, and every
  colour pair passes WCAG 2.2 contrast. They were the separate Seasonal styles plug-in, which is retired.

## 1.1.0 (11 November 2026)

### Added
- Five more styles: Aurora, Nordic, Sunset, Graphite and Editorial, each in light and dark.
- Gradients, textures and glass: Aurora and Vivid have soft glows, Sunset, Nordic and Frost gradients, Graphite a grid, Bento dots, Nordic and Editorial a paper grain, and Aurora, Sunset and Frost glass panels. Aurora, Sunset, Frost, Bento and Vivid have rounded buttons and fields.
- They also set the side menu, panel headers, the chosen button, count badges, status messages, chart colours and the logo, and Editorial uses serif headings. Every colour pair passes WCAG 2.2 contrast.

### Changed
- Needs Starboard's plug-in API 1.1 (Starboard 0.16.5 or later).

## 1.0.0 (10 November 2026)

### Added
- Eight styles: Midnight, Frost, Bold, Bento, Paper, Forest, Retro and Vivid, each in light and dark.
- install.py, which opens Starboard with the plug-in ready to add.
